How the lab works

Replay the observations

PMMA film telemetry uses points read from Figures 2.8–2.10. SIBAL fabric telemetry approximates the flame tip and base curves in Figure 7.2. Playback interpolates between points, and the chamber illustrates the observed changes in shape.

For the PMMA film, the exact flow schedule before 27 s is unavailable here. The nominal 3 cm/s segment is an illustrative hold. The leading-edge spread rate is shown starting at 11 s, at the first digitized point.

Explore the rod boundary

Equation 6.1 estimates the final oxygen concentration at the boundary for opposed-flow PMMA rods. The shaded area in the lab chart represents conditions above the fitted boundary.

a = (3/2)(U/r)√(r/r₀)
XO₂ = 6 exp(−0.5a) + 0.0167a + 13.4

Use cm and s for U and r. The reference radius r₀ is 0.318 cm. The fit shown spans 0–150 s⁻¹. The interface highlights a small band above the fitted curve as near extinction. The illustrative oscillation uses 1.1 Hz, observed in one near-quench rod test (Figure 6.6).

Model the PMMA film

The live film model interpolates approximate values from NASA’s computed 100 µm curves at 16 and 21 vol% O₂. The reported low-flow cutoffs are 3.5 and 1.5 cm/s, respectively. Intermediate oxygen values are an interpolation. For comparison, the flight experiments in Table 2.1 report approximately 1.0 ± 0.5 cm/s at 21% O₂ and 3.0 ± 0.5 cm/s at 16% O₂.

For thicker films, the app applies the inverse-thickness relation from §2.2.7 as an approximation. Figures 2.11–2.12 describe 1 atm. The operating envelope here focuses on low-flow extinction and ends at 20 cm/s.

Flame shape, color and response timing are illustrative. Relighting requires the Ignite control. The displayed spread rate is the estimated steady rate under the selected conditions.
