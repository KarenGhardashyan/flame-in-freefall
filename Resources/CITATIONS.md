## 3. Academic Foundations & Foundational Literature

* **Monograph:** Law, Chung K. (2006). *Combustion Physics*. Cambridge University Press. ISBN: 978-0521870528.
  * **Section 0.1:** Defense and Space (Spacecraft cabin fire safety, absence of buoyancy, ceiling smoke detector limitations, $O_2 < 15\%$ flammability threshold).
  * **Chapter 6.4:** Droplet Vaporization and Combustion ($d^2$-law, evaporation constants, microgravity spherical symmetry).
  * **Chapter 8:** Limit Phenomena (S-Curve, Damköhler number $Da$, chemical kinetics vs residence time, flammability limits and extinction criteria).
